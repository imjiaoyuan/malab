Principal investigator

Tao Ma
Professor, College of Life Sciences, Sichuan University

Tao Ma received his BSc in 2009 and PhD in 2014 from Lanzhou University. He has been on the faculty at Sichuan University since 2014, where he leads the Plant Evolution and Functional Genomics Team.

The lab studies the evolutionary genomics of Salicaceae and other woody plants, with a focus on sex chromosome evolution, genome evolution and polyploidy, environmental adaptation, and single-cell and spatial omics.

Yuanzhong Jiang
Associate Research Professor, Master's and Doctoral Supervisor
